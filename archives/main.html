All of our old articles from the time period Holy Might became a raiding guild. Previous to "our time" the guild was created December 23, 2004 as a small casual guild with plans to eventually raid. Since that day, we've gone through four changes of leadership and still have some of our original members & raiders. Current officers and leaders are all long time members of the guild, Templaria the oldest of the bunch!

I went looking for that first "omg! we killed Rognog" thread in the forums, but it's long gone (thank god). However, the rest of the articles are still around to haunt us (coughmecough)!
